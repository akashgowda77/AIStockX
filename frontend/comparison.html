Understanding AI Metrics & Charts

Master the technical importance of each model evaluation score and visual analysis tool.

Prediction Accuracy

Higher is Better
Accuracy % = max(0, 100 - MAPE)
What it Measures

The overall percentage closeness of the AI model's closing price forecasts relative to actual market closing prices.

💡 Investor Takeaway: Provides an instant 0–100% score. A rating of 95%+ means the model's price forecasts were consistently within a 5% error margin.

Direction Accuracy

Higher is Better
Trend Match % = (Correct Up/Down / Total Days) * 100
What it Measures

Calculates how frequently the model correctly predicts whether tomorrow's price will move UP (Bullish) or DOWN (Bearish).

💡 Investor Takeaway: Crucial for trading strategies! Getting the price trend direction right is often far more profitable than predicting exact dollar amounts.

RMSE (Root Mean Squared Error)

Lower is Better
RMSE = √( Σ(y_actual - y_pred)² / N )
What it Measures

Measures standard deviation of residuals, squaring price errors to heavily penalize large, unexpected prediction blunders.

💡 Investor Takeaway: Acts as an 'outlier detector'. A high RMSE warns you that the model occasionally makes major, unpredictable price mistakes.

MAE (Mean Absolute Error)

Lower is Better
MAE = (1 / N) * Σ |y_actual - y_pred|
What it Measures

Calculates the straightforward linear dollar amount the prediction was off by, treating all error sizes equally.

💡 Investor Takeaway: Real-dollar benchmark. An MAE of $1.50 means predictions are on average off by exactly $1.50 per share.

MAPE (Mean Absolute % Error)

Lower is Better
MAPE = (100% / N) * Σ | (y_actual - y_pred) / y_actual |
What it Measures

Normalizes dollar error into a percentage relative to the stock's actual price magnitude.

💡 Investor Takeaway: Scale-invariant metric. Allows fair comparison of prediction accuracy between $10 penny stocks and $1,000 tech stocks.

R² Score (Goodness of Fit)

Scale: 0.0 to 1.0
R² = 1 - [ Σ(y - y_pred)² / Σ(y - y_mean)² ]
What it Measures

Quantifies the proportion of price variance explained by input technical features compared to a simple historical mean line.

💡 Investor Takeaway: Statistical fit grade. 1.0 is a perfect fit; 0 means the model performs no better than guessing the historical average price.

Performance Radar Web

Radar Chart
Visualization Purpose

Plots a model's score across Accuracy, Direction, Confidence, and AI Score on a 360-degree radial grid.

🔍 How to Read: The model whose shaded color area covers more surface area of the web is the superior overall model.

Error Metrics Bar Chart

Grouped Bar
Visualization Purpose

Compares raw error figures (RMSE, MAE, MAPE) between Linear Regression and LSTM models side-by-side.

🔍 How to Read: Since these are error values, shorter bars mean smaller errors and higher prediction accuracy.

Stock Price Line Chart

Area Line
Visualization Purpose

Visualizes historical daily closing prices along with model prediction trajectory over time.

🔍 How to Read: An upward trend slope signifies Bullish growth; a downward slope indicates Bearish decline.

RSI Oscillator (0-100)

Momentum Line
Visualization Purpose

Tracks buying and selling speed bounded on a fixed scale of 0 to 100 with threshold reference lines.

🔍 How to Read: Above 70 = Overbought (likely price drop). Below 30 = Oversold (likely price bounce).

MACD & Histogram

Crossover Bars
Visualization Purpose

Measures short-term vs long-term exponential moving average convergence and divergence.

🔍 How to Read: Green bars above 0 indicate growing Bullish momentum; red bars below zero indicate Bearish weakness.

Bollinger Bands Envelope

Band Envelope
Visualization Purpose

Plots a 20-day SMA middle line bounded by upper and lower bands set 2 standard deviations away.

🔍 How to Read: Expanding bands signal high market volatility. Price touching outer bands suggests extreme price extension.
